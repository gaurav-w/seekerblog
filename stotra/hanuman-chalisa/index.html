The Chālīsā is in Awadhi, spelled here as Gita Press and most Hindi-belt booklets print it — बरनउँ रघुबर, not the Sanskritised वरणौ रघुवर. Tamil has no separate letters for voiced and aspirated consonants, so க covers k, kh, g and gh alike; that is the script, not an error.
