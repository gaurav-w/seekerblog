The deathless witness, lion of courage, and supreme protector

अजो दुर्मर्षणः शास्ता विश्रुतात्मा सुरारिहा ॥ २२ ॥

What Verse 22 is showing us

One devotional arc: Lord Vishnu is the deathless, timeless reality untouched by mortality (Amrityuh), watching the entire universe as the all-seeing cosmic witness (Sarvadrik). He rises with lion-hearted valor to defend innocence (Simhah), harmonizing actions with their destined fruits (Sandhata), and remaining ever united with His devotees in unbroken love (Sandhiman). Standing as the unshakeable rock of truth amidst changing ages (Sthirah), He is unborn and eternal (Ajah), invincible against every shadow of darkness (Durmarshanah). As the supreme lawgiver and compassionate teacher of dharma (Shasta), His boundless glory is praised throughout all sacred scriptures (Vishrutatma) as the ultimate redeemer who vanquishes every foe of righteousness (Surarihah).
