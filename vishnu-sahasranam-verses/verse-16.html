What Verse 16 is showing us

One devotional arc: Lord Vishnu shines with self-luminous cosmic brilliance (Bhrajishnuh). In the sacred mystery of life, He is the nourishing food that sustains existence (Bhojanam) and the conscious experiencer enjoying all offerings (Bhokta). Forbearing and patient with all mortal frailties (Sahishnuh), He was present at creation's first dawn (Jagadadijah). Completely pure and untouched by any flaw (Anaghah), He is triumphant victory itself (Vijayah) and the conqueror of every seeking heart (Jeta). He remains the womb and resting place of the universe (Vishvayonih), dwelling within our bodies life after life as our eternal companion (Punarvasuh).

The ten names in Verse 16

Name 1

भ्राजिष्णुः

Bhrajishnuh

Radiance without source, self-luminous and brilliant.

स्वयं प्रकाशमान और देदीप्यमान तेज स्वरूप

Name 2

भोजनम्

Bhojanam

The sacred nourishment that sustains all living beings.

समस्त जीवों का पोषण और भोग्य पदार्थ

Name 3

भोक्ता

Bhokta

The conscious soul who receives and enjoys all offerings.

यज्ञ और भक्ति को स्वीकार करने वाले भोक्ता

Name 4

सहिष्णुः

Sahishnuh

The patient and deeply forgiving Lord of forbearance.

सबकी भूलों को क्षमा करने और सहने वाले

Name 5

जगदादिजः

Jagadadijah

Born and manifest at the very dawn of creation.

सृष्टि के आरंभ में सर्वप्रथम प्रकट होने वाले

Name 6

अनघः

Anaghah

Spotless and untainted by any trace of sin.

सर्वथा निष्पाप और निर्दोष

Name 7

विजयः

Vijayah

Triumphant victory and righteousness personified.

साक्षात विजय स्वरूप

Name 8

जेता

Jeta

The gentle conqueror of every devotee's heart.

सबके हृदयों को जीत लेने वाले

Name 9

विश्वयोनिः

Vishvayonih

The wellspring and resting home of the cosmos.

संपूर्ण जगत के उद्गम और आश्रय

Name 10

पुनर्वसुः

Punarvasuh

He who dwells again and again in every embodiment.

पुनः पुनः अंतरात्मा में निवास करने वाले
