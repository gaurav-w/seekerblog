What Verse 7 is showing us

One devotional arc: Though He cannot be grasped by mortal grasp alone (Agraahyah), He is eternal and constant through every age (Shaashvatah). As Krishna (Krishnah), the all-attractive one, He pulls every seeking heart toward Himself, gazing upon the world with red lotus-like eyes of compassion (Lohitaakshah). In cosmic dissolution He draws everything peacefully back into Himself (Pratardanah). Radiant in boundless abundance (Prabhootah) and supporting all quarters of space (Trikakubdhaama), He is the purest of all purifiers (Pavitram) and the highest auspiciousness (Mangalam Param).

The nine names in Verse 7

Name 1

अग्राह्यः

Agraahyah

He who cannot be seized or grasped by the senses alone.

जिन्हें केवल इंद्रियों से पकड़ा या जाना नहीं जा सकता

Name 2

शाश्वतः

Shaashvatah

The eternal, timeless, and unchanging Lord.

जो सदा शाश्वत और अपरिवर्तनीय रहते हैं

Name 3

कृष्णः

Krishnah

The all-attractive Lord who draws every soul toward Himself.

जो सभी जीवों को अपने आनंदमय स्वरूप की ओर खींचते हैं

Name 4

लोहिताक्षः

Lohitaakshah

He with eyes glowing tenderly like red lotus blossoms.

लाल कमल के समान करुणामय नेत्रों वाले

Name 5

प्रतर्दनः

Pratardanah

The supreme dissolver who gently draws creation back home.

प्रलय काल में सब कुछ अपने में समेटने वाले

Name 6

प्रभूतः

Prabhootah

He who is manifest in boundless majesty and fullness.

जो असीम ऐश्वर्य और महानता में प्रकट होते हैं

Name 7

त्रिककुब्धाम

Trikakubdhaama

The sanctuary and support of all quarters of the cosmos.

ब्रह्मांड की सभी दिशाओं के आधार और धाम

Name 8

पवित्रम्

Pavitram

The supreme purifier who sanctifies everything He touches.

जो स्वयं अत्यंत पावन हैं और सबको पवित्र करते हैं

Name 9

मङ्गलं परम्

Mangalam Param

The pinnacle of all auspiciousness, goodness, and grace.

सर्वोच्च कल्याणकारी, शुभ और मंगलमय स्वरूप
