Vishnu Sahasranama · Verse 4

The all-inclusive, auspicious, and unwavering ground of existence

सम्भवो भावनो भर्ता प्रभवः प्रभुरीश्वरः ॥ ४ ॥

The fourth verse unfolds twelve names of Lord Vishnu, revealing how the unchanging source of all life lovingly descends, sustains every breath, and governs the cosmic dance.

What Verse 4 is showing us

One devotional arc: Lord Vishnu is praised as everything in existence (Sarvah), the auspicious destroyer of suffering and sorrow (Sharvah), and the essence of pure peace and goodness (Shivah). He is the immovable pillar of truth (Sthanuh) and the primeval origin of all creatures (Bhutadih), an inexhaustible spiritual treasure (Nidhiravyayah). By His own sovereign grace He descends as divine incarnation (Sambhavah), showers boundless blessings upon His seekers (Bhavanah), faithfully bears and sustains life (Bharta), shines as the primordial source (Prabhavah), stands as all-powerful sovereign (Prabhuh), and reigns as supreme controller (Ishvarah).

Name 1

सर्वः

Sarvah

He who is everything and all-inclusive.

वह जो स्वयं समस्त चराचर जगत है

Name 2

शर्वः

Sharvah

The auspicious destroyer of darkness and sorrows.

दुखों और पापों का संहार करने वाले

Name 3

शिवः

Shivah

The eternally pure, auspicious, and benevolent.

जो सदैव परम पवित्र और कल्याणकारी हैं

Name 4

स्थाणुः

Sthanuh

The firm, motionless, and unshakable ground of truth.

सदा स्थिर, अचल और अडिग रहने वाले

Name 5

भूतादिः

Bhutadih

The primeval source and origin of all beings.

समस्त जीवों और तत्वों के मूल उद्गम

Name 6

निधिरव्ययः

Nidhiravyayah

The inexhaustible cosmic treasure.

कभी न समाप्त होने वाली अक्षय निधि

Name 7

सम्भवः

Sambhavah

He who manifests through His own free divine will.

जो अपनी इच्छा से अवतार लेकर प्रकट होते हैं

Name 8

भावनः

Bhavanah

He who generously blesses and nurtures devotees.

भक्तों का कल्याण और पोषण करने वाले

Name 9

भर्ता

Bharta

The loyal supporter and sustainer of creation.

सृष्टि का भार उठाने वाले पालनहार

Name 10

प्रभवः

Prabhavah

The exalted womb and source of all power.

समस्त शक्तियों और पदार्थों के उद्भव स्थल

Name 11

प्रभुः

Prabhuh

The Almighty Lord capable of all things.

सर्वशक्तिमान और सर्वसमर्थ स्वामी

Name 12

ईश्वरः

Ishvarah

The supreme sovereign controller of the cosmos.

संपूर्ण ब्रह्मांड के परम नियंता
