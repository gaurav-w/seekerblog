Vishnu Sahasranama · Verse 1

The Lord who is the universe, within and around us

विश्वं विष्णुर्वषट्कारो भूतभव्यभवत्प्रभुः ।
भूतकृद्भूतभृद्भावो भूतात्मा भूतभावनः ॥ १ ॥

The first verse introduces nine names of Lord Vishnu. Together, they move from the vastness of the universe to the quiet presence of the divine within every being.

What Verse 1 is showing us

One devotional arc: Lord Vishnu is named as the whole universe (Vishvam), the presence pervading it (Vishnuh), and the sacred purpose behind our offerings (Vashatkarah). The verse then turns to time, creation, support, being, the inner Self, and the force that helps life flourish. It is an invitation to meet the divine not only in a temple, but in time, work, life, and the heart.

Creator and shaper of all beings.

भूतभृत्

Bhutabhrit

The sustainer who supports all.

जो सबका पोषण करते हैं

भावः

Bhavah

Absolute existence and pure being.

जो स्वयं अस्तित्व है

Name 8

भूतात्मा

The inner Self of all beings.

सभी जीवों की अंतरात्मा

भूतभावनः

Bhutabhavanah

The nurturing force that helps all beings flourish.
