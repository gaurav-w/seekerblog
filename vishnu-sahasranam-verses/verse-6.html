What Verse 6 is showing us

One devotional arc: Lord Vishnu stands beyond all human measurement or mental limitation (Aprameyah), while remaining the direct master who harmonizes our senses and minds (Hrishikeshah). From His cosmic navel blooms the lotus of creation (Padmanabhah), as He reigns supreme over the immortals (Amaraprabhuh). He is the supreme architect who fashions every detail of the universe (Vishvakarma), the primordial seer and lawgiver (Manuh), and the divine sculptor shaping subtle forms (Tvashta). Immensely vast and all-enveloping (Sthavishtah), He remains the timeless, unmoving, and unshakable truth (Sthaviro Dhruvah).

The nine names in Verse 6

Name 1

अप्रमेयः

Aprameyah

He who cannot be measured or grasped by logic alone.

जो तर्क और प्रमाणों से परे, अपरिमेय हैं

Name 2

हृषीकेशः

Hrishikeshah

The sovereign master and ruler of the senses.

इंद्रियों के परम स्वामी और मार्गदर्शक

Name 3

पद्मनाभः

Padmanabhah

He from whose navel springs the lotus of creation.

जिनकी नाभि से सृष्टि का कमल प्रकट होता है

Name 4

अमरप्रभुः

Amaraprabhuh

The Lord and sovereign guardian of the immortals.

देवताओं और अमरों के स्वामी

Name 5

विश्वकर्मा

Vishvakarma

The master builder and architect of the universe.

संपूर्ण ब्रह्मांड के रचयिता और शिल्पकार

Name 6

मनुः

Manuh

The supreme thinker, source of wisdom and sacred law.

महान विचारक और ज्ञान के स्रोत

Name 7

त्वष्टा

Tvashta

The master sculptor who crafts every subtle form.

सभी रूपों को गढ़ने और आकार देने वाले

Name 8

स्थविष्ठः

Sthavishtah

Supremely vast, immense, and all-encompassing.

अत्यंत विशाल और विराट स्वरूप वाले

Name 9

स्थविरो ध्रुवः

Sthaviro Dhruvah

The ancient, immovable, and unchanging reality.

सबसे प्राचीन, स्थिर और कभी न बदलने वाले सत्य
