What Verse 21 is showing us

One devotional arc: Lord Vishnu shines as the primordial, supreme effulgence kindling sun and stars (Marichih), subduing all internal and external turmoil with serene mastery (Damanah). In pure, spotless majesty He is the sovereign swan discerning truth from illusion (Hamsah), bearing devotees across existence upon beautiful golden wings (Suparnah), and resting serenely upon the master of sacred serpents (Bhujagottamah). From His radiant golden navel flows the light of creation (Hiranyanabhah), embodying the perfection of sacred austerity (Sutapah). As the cosmic lotus blossoms from His depths to awaken Brahma (Padmanabhah), He protects and nurtures all beings as the eternal father and guardian of life (Prajapatih).
