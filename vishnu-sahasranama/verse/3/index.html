Vishnu Sahasranama · Verse 3

The master of yoga, protector of devotees, and supreme person

योगो योगविदां नेता प्रधानपुरुषेश्वरः ।
नारसिंहवपुः श्रीमान् केशवः पुरुषोत्तमः ॥ ३ ॥

The third verse presents seven profound names of Lord Vishnu, transitioning from the path of inner union to the magnificent protector who guards and uplifts all beings.

What Verse 3 is showing us

One devotional arc: Lord Vishnu is yoga itself (Yogah), the guide who leads seekers toward liberation (Yogavidam Neta), and the supreme sovereign over both material nature and the individual soul (Pradhana-Purusheshvarah). He takes the fierce, loving form of the Man-Lion to protect His devotee (Narasimhavapuh), remains eternally accompanied by divine grace (Shriman), holds the beautiful rays of cosmic consciousness (Keshavah), and stands as the supreme being beyond all creation (Purushottamah).

The seven names in Verse 3

Name 1

योगः

Yogah

The path and the goal of divine union.

योग और मिलन के साक्षात् स्वरूप

Name 2

योगविदां नेता

Yogavidam Neta

The supreme guide for all who seek spiritual union.

योगियों और साधकों के मार्गदर्शक

Name 3

प्रधानपुरुषेश्वरः

Pradhana-Purusheshvarah

The sovereign ruler over nature and the soul.

प्रकृति और पुरुष के परम स्वामी

Name 4

नारसिंहवपुः

Narasimhavapuh

He who manifested the awe-inspiring Man-Lion form.

नरसिंह रूप धारण करने वाले रक्षक प्रभु

Name 5

श्रीमान्

Shriman

The embodiment of divine grace, beauty, and auspiciousness.

लक्ष्मी जी से सुशोभित और सर्व-ऐश्वर्यवान

Name 6

केशवः

Keshavah

The source of creation, radiance, and divine harmony.

सुंदर केशों वाले, सृष्टि के आदि कारण

Name 7

पुरुषोत्तमः

Purushottamah

The Supreme Being, highest of all.

सर्वश्रेष्ठ और परम पुरुष
