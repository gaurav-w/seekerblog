What Verse 25 is showing us

One devotional arc: The Konark stone chariot wheel turns as the wheel of days, with Lord Vishnu standing unmoved at its living hub as Avartanah (Turns Life's Wheel). The Konark wheel's rim blurs while the stone hub stays still, showing the Lord's inner Self never turns as Nivrittatma (The Unmoved Self). Coastal cloud wraps the Konark sun, yet Lord Vishnu's outline still glows, hidden but present as Samvritah (The Hidden Lord). Dawn light through the Konark portal dissolves every stone-shadow into gold, showing the Lord who crushes all darkness without violence as Sampramardanah (Crushes All Darkness). The Konark sundial shadow completes its turn as daylight folds across the temple like a golden cloth, showing the Lord who rolls day into night as Ahahsamvartakah (Rolls Day and Night). The sacred homa fire carries offerings upward before Lord Vishnu, showing that the sacrificial flame is His own divine form as Vahnih (Sacred Offering Fire). The Chandrabhaga sea wind lifts Konark's flags and incense, the Lord's unseen breath moving through the temple as Anilah (The Living Breath). The sea wind settles the Konark earth-temple upon Ananta-Shesha, Lord Vishnu bearing the world as Dharanidharah (Bearer of the Earth).
