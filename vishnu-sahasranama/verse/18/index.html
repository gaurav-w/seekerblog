Vishnu Sahasranama · Verse 18

The divine healer, master of yoga, and gentle immense strength

अतीन्द्रियो महामायो महोत्साहो महाबलः ॥ १८ ॥

What Verse 18 is showing us

One devotional arc: Lord Vishnu is the ultimate goal of all Vedic knowledge (Vedyah) and the supreme divine physician whose nectar heals all worldly ailments (Vaidyah). In the midst of cosmic activity He rests in unbroken meditative stillness (Sadayogi), yet rises with decisive strength to protect truth and overcome powerful obstacles (Viraha). Inseparably united with Sri Lakshmi in Vaikuntha (Madhavah), He pours forth divine sweetness that softens seeking hearts (Madhuh). Transcending the grasp of outer senses (Atindriyah), He weaves and sees through the vast tapestry of cosmic illusion (Mahamayah), upholding all worlds with tireless sustaining power (Mahotsahah) and effortless, gentle might that can lift a mountain on a single finger (Mahabalah).

The ten names in Verse 18

Name 1

वेद्यः

Vedyah

The ultimate goal and destination of all sacred knowledge.

समस्त ज्ञान और वेदों के अंतिम गंतव्य

Name 2

वैद्यः

Vaidyah

The divine physician who cures all worldly suffering with the nectar of immortality.

दिव्य चिकित्सक, जो जन्म-संसार के रोगों का नाश करते हैं

Name 3

सदायोगी

Sadayogi

Ever established in unbroken inner yoga and meditative stillness.

सदा अपने आत्मस्वरूप और योग में स्थित रहने वाले

Name 4

वीरहा

Viraha

Subduer of powerful demonic forces and obstacles to dharma.

प्रबल असुरों और अज्ञान का संहार करने वाले

Name 5

माधवः

Madhavah

Beloved and inseparable Lord of Goddess Lakshmi.

माँ लक्ष्मी के प्रिय और अविभाज्य स्वामी

Name 6

मधुः

Madhuh

Divine sweetness and nectar that softens every seeking heart.

दिव्य माधुर्य और अमृत स्वरूप, जो हृदय को शांति देते हैं

Name 7

अतीन्द्रियः

Atindriyah

Beyond the grasp of physical senses, realized through inner contemplation.

इंद्रियों से परे, अंतर्दृष्टि से अनुभव किए जाने वाले

Name 8

महामायः

Mahamayah

Master and weaver of the cosmic veil of Maya.

समस्त सृष्टि की माया के स्वामी और नियंत्रक

Name 9

महोत्साहः

Mahotsahah

Endowed with tireless, continuous sustaining energy that never pauses.

अथक और निरंतर सक्रिय रहने वाली पालन-शक्ति

Name 10

महाबलः

Mahabalah

Possessor of gentle, immense strength that upholds creation effortlessly.

कोमलता सहित अपार बल धारण करने वाले
