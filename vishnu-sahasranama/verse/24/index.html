What Verse 24 is showing us

One devotional arc: Lord Vishnu steps forward into the darkness, lighting the sacred path for every climbing pilgrim as Agranih (The Foremost Guide). Like a tender shepherd, Lord Vishnu gathers and protects all living beings along the sacred journey as Gramanih (Leader of All Beings). Mother Lakshmi eternally abides on Lord Srinivasa's golden chest, blessing the sanctum with unbroken grace as Shriman (Endowed with Divine Glory). The temple Tulabharam scale steadies in flawless balance, showing that divine truth and sincere devotion outweigh all worldly power as Nyayah (Justice and Righteousness). Lord Vishnu calmly steers the wheel of cosmic time, guiding all created worlds along the path of dharma as Neta (The Supreme Governor). The refreshing mountain wind on Venkatadri carries Lord Vishnu's animating breath of life into every being as Samiranah (The Breath of Life). Lord Vishnu's cosmic Purusha form expands beyond the golden temple tower, crowned across all dimensions as Sahasramurdha (The Thousand-Headed Lord). Lord Vishnu dwells as the single, loving soul within every stone, star, and living pilgrim as Vishvatma (Soul of the Universe). Lord Srinivasa's large, unblinking lotus eyes watch over every seeking soul with boundless compassion as Sahasrakshah (The All-Seeing Witness). Lord Vishnu's thousand lotus feet sustain all worlds and tenderly carry forward every weary seeker on the path as Sahasrapat (The All-Pervading Feet).
