What Verse 2 is showing us

One devotional arc: Lord Vishnu is celebrated as the ever-pure Self (Pootatma) and the supreme unconditioned reality (Paramatma). He is the ultimate destination where freed souls find resting peace (Muktanam Parama Gatih), remaining inexhaustible and imperishable (Avyayah). Resting as the indwelling spirit (Purushah), He watches existence as the silent witness (Sakshi), knows the depths of every body and mind (Kshetragnyah), and remains forever indestructible (Aksharah).

The eight names in Verse 2

Name 1

पूतात्मा

Pootatma

The Pure Self, untouched by worldly impurities.

पवित्र आत्मा, जो संसार के विकारों से अछूते हैं

Name 2

परमात्मा

Paramatma

The Supreme Soul and ultimate reality.

सर्वोच्च आत्मा और परम सत्य

Name 3

मुक्तानां परमा गतिः

Muktanam Parama Gatih

The ultimate goal and refuge of liberated souls.

मुक्त जीवों की परम गति और अंतिम आश्रय

Name 4

अव्ययः

Avyayah

The inexhaustible and imperishable One.

जो कभी नष्ट नहीं होता, अविनाशी प्रभु

Name 5

पुरुषः

Purushah

The indwelling spirit residing in all hearts.

सभी के हृदय में निवास करने वाले अंतर्यामी

Name 6

साक्षी

Sakshi

The eternal, unattached witness of everything.

सब कुछ देखने वाले निष्पक्ष साक्षी

Name 7

क्षेत्रज्ञः

Kshetragnyah

The knower of the field of body and mind.

क्षेत्र (शरीर और मन) को पूर्णतः जानने वाले

Name 8

अक्षरः

Aksharah

The indestructible and changeless truth.

अक्षर सत्य, जिसका कभी क्षय नहीं होता
