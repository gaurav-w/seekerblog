Vishnu Sahasranama · Verse 10

The ultimate refuge, wheel of time, and all-seeing witness

अहः संवत्सरो व्यालः प्रत्ययः सर्वदर्शनः ॥ १० ॥

The tenth verse presents ten names of Lord Vishnu, unveiling the Lord as the safe haven of all beings, the soul of time and light, and the eternal witness watching over creation.

What Verse 10 is showing us

One devotional arc: Lord Vishnu is the divine ruler of celestial deities (Sureshah) and the ultimate refuge where every weary soul finds shelter (Sharanam), blessing seekers with supreme spiritual bliss (Sharma). He is the primordial seed from which the cosmos sprouts (Vishvaretah) and the loving source of all living beings (Prajabhavah). Shining as the bright dawn of knowledge (Ahah) and the cyclical rhythm of time (Samvatsarah), He is elusive to the ego yet ever accessible to devotion (Vyalah). As pure consciousness and realization (Pratyayah), He stands as the all-seeing, omniscient witness of reality (Sarvadarshanah).

Name 1

सुरेशः

Sureshah

Lord of the celestials and protector of light.

देवों के देव और प्रकाश के रक्षक

Name 2

शरणम्

Sharanam

The ultimate refuge and safe sanctuary for all.

सब जीवों का परम आश्रय और शरण

Name 3

शर्म

Sharma

Supreme spiritual bliss and deep inner peace.

परम आनंद और असीम शांति का स्वरूप

Name 4

विश्वरेताः

Vishvaretah

The primordial seed from which the universe springs.

संपूर्ण ब्रह्मांड का मूल बीज

Name 5

प्रजाभवः

Prajabhavah

The origin and father from whom all creatures arise.

सभी प्राणियों के जन्मदाता

Name 6

अहः

Ahah

The radiant day, dispelling darkness with divine light.

प्रकाशमय दिन, जो अंधकार को दूर करते हैं

Name 7

संवत्सरः

Samvatsarah

Time Himself, guiding the cyclical seasons of life.

काल और संवत्सर स्वरूप, जो समय को गति देते हैं

Name 8

व्यालः

Vyalah

Elusive to arrogance, yet warmly welcoming to love.

अहंकारियों के लिए अगम्य, पर भक्तों के लिए सुलभ

Name 9

प्रत्ययः

Pratyayah

Pure conscious awareness and absolute realization.

परम ज्ञान और चेतना का प्रत्यक्ष अनुभव

Name 10

सर्वदर्शनः

Sarvadarshanah

The all-seeing, omniscient cosmic witness.

सब कुछ देखने वाले सर्वदर्शी साक्षी
